Our Story

We Help Authors Get Noticed

Ink House Media is a small team of individuals who figured out a way to help authors increase their visibility and sales. We are not storytellers and we are not filmmakers. We are marketers, designers, and editors who focus on one job. Getting your book in front of more people.

We noticed that independent authors, many with brilliant books, were being buried under generic cover graphics and static promotional posts. At the same time, major publishers were investing in video, dominating social feeds, and turning passive scrollers into passionate readers.

So we built a studio that closes that gap. We produce video and marketing assets that make your book look like a major release, and we do it at a price that makes sense for independent authors.

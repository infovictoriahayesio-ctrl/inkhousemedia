Ink House Media is a creative production studio specializing in cinematic book trailers, author branding, and book marketing assets — trusted by authors from around the world since 2022.

Our Story

Where Literature Meets Cinema

Ink House Media was founded in 2022 by passionate storytellers and filmmakers who believed every book deserved a visual identity as compelling as the words on the page.

We noticed that independent authors — many with brilliant, cinematic stories — were being buried under generic cover graphics and static promotional posts. Meanwhile, major publishers were investing in video, dominating social feeds, and turning passive scrollers into passionate readers.

So we built a studio that bridges the gap. We bring Hollywood-grade production values to independent publishing — and we do it at a price that makes sense for authors.

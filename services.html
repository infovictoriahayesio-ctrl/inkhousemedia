Pricing FAQs

Teaser trailers are short (15–60s), atmospheric, and hook-driven — designed for social media scroll-stopping. Cinematic trailers are longer (45–120s), story-driven, and include narration, animation, and original scoring. They're the flagship asset for a full launch campaign.

We take a 50% deposit to begin work and the remaining 50% on final delivery. We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfer. Payment plans are available for combo and full marketing packages.

Every package includes revision rounds (1 to unlimited depending on tier). We share a first cut for feedback before final production — so you're never locked into something you don't love.

The 50% deposit is non-refundable once production begins (as it covers scripting, licensing, and initial edit). If you cancel before production starts, we refund the deposit minus a 10% administrative fee.

Absolutely. You can upgrade from Teaser to Cinematic or add Combo services at any point. We'll credit what you've already paid toward the upgrade.

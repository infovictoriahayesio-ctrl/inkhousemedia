Service 01

Book Trailers

The flagship service — cinematic short films that translate your story into motion and stop the scroll in under 3 seconds.

A book trailer is not a slideshow of your cover. It's a 30–90 second cinematic piece built like a film trailer — with pacing, tension, music, typography, and a clear emotional arc. It exists to do one job: make a viewer feel your book before they ever read a page.

We read your manuscript, identify the strongest hook, and build a trailer that sells the feeling of your story — the atmosphere, the conflict, the promise. Then we deliver it in every format you need so it can be deployed across TikTok, Reels, YouTube, your website, your email list, and paid ads.

🎬

What You Get

Script, storyboard, cinematic edit, licensed footage, sound design, voiceover (if included), on-screen typography, color grade, and multi-format exports (vertical, square, horizontal).

⚙️

How It Works

You share your book and vision. We write a cinematic script and storyboard, produce the visuals and sound, send a first cut for review, revise, then deliver final files optimized for every platform.

📈

How It Helps Authors

Video content is shared up to 1200% more than text. A trailer gives you a permanent, reusable marketing asset that increases engagement, grows your audience, and drives sales on every channel you use.

⚡

Teaser Trailer

15–60 seconds. Fast, atmospheric, mystery-driven. Perfect for social media hooks, pre-launch buzz, and paid ads.

🎞️

Cinematic Trailer

45–90 seconds. Full story-driven production with narration, animation, and original score. Your flagship marketing asset.

🔁

Series Trailer

For multi-book authors and trilogies — a single trailer that sells the entire series and builds long-term readership.

Service 02

Author Branding

Build an author identity as memorable as your stories — so readers recognize you instantly on any shelf.

Readers don't just fall in love with books — they fall in love with authors. Your brand is the visual and emotional signature that travels with every book you release, every post you publish, and every email you send.

We create a cohesive visual and editorial identity for authors: logo, color palette, typography, book cover templates, social media kit, and a style guide that keeps everything consistent across years and platforms.

✒️

What You Get

Author logo or wordmark, brand color palette, typography system, book cover template, social media templates, email signature, and a brand guide PDF.

⚙️

How It Works

We run a short brand discovery (genre, audience, tone, influences), then design 2–3 concept directions. You pick one, we refine it, and deliver a full brand kit with everything ready to use.

📈

How It Helps Authors

A strong author brand increases recognition, builds reader loyalty, and makes every future launch easier. It's the difference between "someone who wrote a book" and "an author with a following."

Service 03

Social Media Assets

Short-form, platform-native video content engineered for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, X, and Facebook.

One trailer is not enough. Modern authors need a steady stream of short-form content to stay visible. We package each trailer into a full content set — hooks, vertical cuts, text overlays, quote cards, and cover reveals — ready to post on a schedule.

Every asset is engineered for the algorithm: strong hook in the first 1.5 seconds, bold captions, native aspect ratios, and music that matches platform trends without copyright issues.

📱

What You Get

Up to 10 vertical cut-downs per trailer, 5–10 quote/teaser cards, cover reveal loop, author intro clip, and captions/subtitles ready for each platform.

⚙️

How It Works

We start from your trailer (or a fresh cut), then design platform-native versions with the correct aspect ratio, safe areas, caption placement, and music. Delivered in a shared folder ready to schedule.

📈

How It Helps Authors

Consistent short-form content is the single biggest driver of organic book discovery. Authors who post weekly see 3–5× more profile visits, follower growth, and click-throughs to their book pages.

Service 04

Book Launch Campaigns

A full pre-launch, launch-week, and post-launch strategy engineered to maximize day-one momentum and sustained sales.

A launch is not a single day — it's a 4 to 6 week runway. Amazon's algorithm rewards books that spike in the first 48 hours after release, so a coordinated push is what gets you into "also bought" lists and category best-sellers.

We plan and produce the entire campaign: trailer, email sequence, social content, ad creative, press kit, and a launch schedule you can follow day by day.

Pre-Launch

Weeks −6 to −1
  • Teaser trailer release
  • Email list warm-up sequence
  • ARC reader recruitment
  • Cover reveal post set
  • Pre-order push content

Launch Week

Days 0 to 7
  • Full trailer drop
  • Daily social content
  • Amazon ad creative set
  • Newsletter blast sequence
  • Review request funnel

Post-Launch

Weeks +1 to +8
  • Sustained content schedule
  • Reader community outreach
  • Review-driven ad refresh
  • Long-tail keyword content
  • Performance reporting

Service 05

Animation Services

Custom animated sequences that bring characters, worlds, and concepts to life — for trailers, series intros, and animated shorts.

Sometimes live-action stock footage isn't enough. For fantasy, sci-fi, children's books, and complex non-fiction, custom animation gives you visuals that match your story exactly — no compromises, no "close enough" clips.

We produce 2D motion graphics, illustrated animation, character animation, and cinematic title sequences. Every frame is built from your book's world, not a template.

🎞️

What You Get

Custom animated sequences, illustrated scenes, animated typography, character motion, and full integration into your trailer or as standalone animated shorts.

⚙️

How It Works

We storyboard each animated beat, design the visual style (matching your book's tone), then animate, composite, and integrate with sound design and music.

📈

How It Helps Authors

Animation makes your story instantly recognizable and shareable. It's especially powerful for children's books, fantasy worlds, and any story where stock footage can't capture the world you built.

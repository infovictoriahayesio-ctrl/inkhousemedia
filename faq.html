Category 02

Process & Timeline

1. Intake. You share your manuscript, genre, goals, and vision through our quick form.

2. Reading & analysis. Our creative team reads your book and identifies the strongest hook and emotional beats.

3. Recommendation. We propose the ideal trailer style, package, and creative direction.

4. Script & storyboard. We write a cinematic script and storyboard for your approval.

5. Production. Motion graphics, sound design, voiceover, and editing.

6. Review & delivery. You review the first cut, we revise, then deliver final files in all formats.

Timelines depend on the package:

Teaser trailers: 5–7 business days

Cinematic trailers: 10–14 business days

Premium / animated trailers: 14–21 business days

Combo & full marketing: Scoped per project — typically 3–6 weeks total.

Rush delivery is available on request (+40% of package price).
